Foundational Research & Concepts

Holographic-Guided Surgical Overlay

Superimposing highly accurate 3D augmented reality models directly onto patient anatomy. This eliminates the need for surgeons to look away from the operative field to separate 2D monitors, reducing cognitive load and increasing surgical precision during complex procedures.

Immersive Surgical Telementoring

Transitioning from 2D teleconferencing to 3D spatial communication for remote medical guidance. By utilizing lightweight wearable AR, expert surgeons can place dynamic, depth-accurate visual cues directly into the exact field of view of a novice surgeon.

Volumetric Medical Imaging

Developing rendering pipelines that seamlessly convert standard hospital DICOM data (MRI/CT scans) into explorable volumetric models. This work empowers medical teams to walk through and inspect patient-specific anatomy in 3D prior to making a single incision.
